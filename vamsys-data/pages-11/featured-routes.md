## Charter of the Week: Phenom 300E launch

**CRX113 / CRX114, Indianapolis Executive (KTYQ) - Dallas Love Field (KDAL), 669 nm.**
Take the newest jet in the fleet, Phenom 300E N300CR, down to Love Field for a client meeting and bring them home the same day. It is a comfortable 1 h 50 m each way: a good first trip to learn the Phenom's Prodigy Touch flight deck and its single-pilot workflow. CJ4, Learjet, Premier, Hawker, Citation X and Mustang are also on the route.
