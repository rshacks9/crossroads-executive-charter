Crossroads Executive & Charter flies 19 fleets from two Indianapolis airports. The rule is simple: light and mid-size jets live at Indianapolis Executive (KTYQ); everything larger lives at Indianapolis International (KIND). Every route is assigned to the fleets that can fly it, so if a fleet appears on a route you can book it.

New: Embraer Phenom 300E

N300CR, the Embraer Phenom 300E (E55P), joined the KTYQ fleet in November 2026. It is the best-selling light jet in the world: single-pilot, 1,950 nm planning range, happy on short runways. It now flies every KTYQ and point-to-point light-jet route within its range, including the mountain airports, the Caribbean and London City.

Airport restrictions we follow

  • Aspen (KASE): 95 ft wingspan limit, so no Gulfstreams; Mustang and Premier stay out of all mountain airports.

  • Teterboro (KTEB): 100,000 lb weight limit, so no VIP airliners.

  • London City (EGLC): steep-approach-approved types only: Mustang, CJ4 and Phenom 300E.

  • A340-600: long-runway airports only (no Van Nuys, Luton or St Maarten).

  • Hawker and Citation X: 5,500 ft runways or longer (so not Key West, Destin, Ohio State or Hilton Head).

Planning ranges are the limits we used to assign routes, with a margin. Always dispatch with SimBrief and check your fuel.
