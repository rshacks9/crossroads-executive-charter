Welcome aboard

Crossroads Executive & Charter flies from two Indianapolis airports across four divisions, with a network of more than 300 routes. This page covers how we operate. The Fleet Guide, Route Guide, Featured Routes, Booking and PIREP Guide and Ranks and Milestones pages go into detail.

Where each aircraft flies from

  • KTYQ, Indianapolis Executive: light and mid-size jets up to Citation X size: Citation Mustang, Premier IA, Learjet 35A, Citation CJ4, Phenom 300E (new), Hawker 800XP and Citation X.

  • KIND, Indianapolis International: everything larger: Citation Longitude, Gulfstream G650 and G700, A220-300, A340-600 and A380 (VIP), E175, E190 and 757-200 (group and sports), and the 777F, MD-11F and A300-600F freighters.

  • Between the bases: CRX733/734 is a short KTYQ-KIND positioning leg for the light jets (maintenance, crew swaps, customs). Use it, or Jumpseat, to change base.

Our four divisions

  • Executive (KTYQ and point-to-point): light and mid-size jets to business airports, ski resorts, Florida, the Caribbean, the Northeast and the West Coast, plus point-to-point shuttles such as Teterboro-Martha's Vineyard, Van Nuys-Las Vegas and Denver Centennial-Aspen. Flights CRX101-199 and CRX701-799.

  • Global Executive (KIND): Longitude, Gulfstreams and VIP-configured A220, A340 and A380 to ski resorts, the coasts, the Caribbean, Hawaii and Europe (London, Paris, Geneva, Zurich, Milan, Madrid, Munich, Nice, Shannon). Flights CRX401-499 and CRX501-599.

  • Group and Sports Charter (KIND): E175, E190 and 757 (plus the A220 and A340 on selected trips) for pro teams, college teams and groups. Flights CRX201-299 and CRX301-399.

  • Cargo Charter (KIND): 777F, MD-11F and A300-600F to Miami, Newark, Oakland, Ontario, Fort Worth Alliance, Mexico City, Anchorage, Stansted, Leipzig and Liege. Flights CRX601-699.

Callsign

Radio callsign CROSSROADS, ICAO code CRX. The flight number is CX plus the same digits (CRX183 is flight CX183). Your booking shows the callsign for each flight.

How a flight works

  1. Book a route in Phoenix that departs from your current location and lists your aircraft.

  2. Start cold and dark at a suitable parking spot, and dispatch with SimBrief from the booking page.

  3. Load the flight plan into the FMS or GPS, then taxi.

  4. Fly with Pegasus ACARS running. Your PIREP files automatically when you arrive.

Flying on VATSIM

Online flying is welcome and encouraged. When you connect to VATSIM as a Crossroads pilot:

  • You are the pilot in command, and you are at the controls. Stay at your desk for the whole connected session. Monitor your frequency and the unicom, and answer ATC yourself.

  • You handle every radio call. Voice or text are both fine; automated or AI-generated radio calls are not. Copilot tools, checklists and autopilots are fine as aids, but a human must stay in the loop and be able to respond immediately.

  • Never leave a connected aircraft unattended, paused or time-accelerated. If you must step away, tell ATC, or disconnect first.

  • Use the right callsign and type: file CRX plus the flight number (for example CRX733), the correct ICAO type and the remark RMK/CROSSROADS VIRTUAL. Say "CROSSROADS" on the radio.

  • Be ready before you connect: charts, ATIS, the filed route loaded and the correct position. Connect at a parking spot, never on a runway or taxiway.

  • Follow the VATSIM Code of Conduct and controller instructions. If you are new, fly an uncontrolled area first, or read the VATSIM Pilot Resource Centre.

Ranks

Promotions are automatic as your hours and accepted flights add up: Cadet (day one), First Officer (10 h, 5 flights), Captain (25 h, 15), Senior Captain (75 h, 40), Charter Captain (150 h, 80), Chief Pilot (300 h, 150).
