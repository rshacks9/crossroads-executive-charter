# Ranks

## Ranks

| Rank | Hours | Accepted PIREPs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cadet | 0 | 0 |
| First Officer | 10 | 5 |
| Captain | 25 | 15 |
| Senior Captain | 75 | 40 |
| Charter Captain | 150 | 80 |
| Chief Pilot | 300 | 150 |

Promotions are automatic as your hours and accepted PIREPs add up. Ranks recognise experience; they do not lock aircraft. Every route lists the fleets allowed to fly it, and every pilot can fly every route from day one.

## Tours and badges

Tours award bonus points and a badge on your profile when you complete every leg. Register for them under Activities in Phoenix.

- **Ski Season Tour 2026-27** (until 15 April 2027, 500 points): KTYQ to Vail/Eagle, Aspen, Jackson Hole and Sun Valley and back, eight legs in any CJ4, Phenom 300E, Learjet 35A, Hawker 800XP or Citation X.
- **Crossroads Circuit** (until 31 December 2027, 300 points): eight short legs out of KTYQ to Louisville, Chicago Midway, Columbus and Nashville and back, each under an hour. Any KTYQ light or mid-size jet; a perfect way to learn a new type.
- **Across the Pond: Transatlantic Executive Tour** (until 30 June 2027, 750 points): KIND to Paris Le Bourget, Geneva and Zurich and back, six legs in the G650, G700 or a VIP A340 or A380.

## Milestones worth chasing

- **First flight:** any CRX route from KTYQ or KIND.
- **All four divisions:** an executive flight, a group or sports charter, a VIP airliner trip and a cargo run.
- **Mountain flying:** Aspen, Vail, Jackson Hole, Sun Valley, Telluride (Montrose), Steamboat (Hayden), Big Sky (Bozeman) and Lake Tahoe (Truckee).
- **Across the pond:** a Gulfstream or VIP airliner trip to Europe.
- **Game day:** fly a team charter to a pro or college game city.
- **Smooth operator:** keep your landings gentle; the landing rate is recorded on every PIREP.

*Crossroads Executive & Charter is a fictional virtual airline for flight simulation.*
