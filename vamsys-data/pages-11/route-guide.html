Every Crossroads flight uses the callsign CRX plus a three-digit number, and the flight number CX plus the same digits (CRX183 is flight CX183). Odd numbers fly outbound from Indianapolis or the first city of a pair; even numbers fly the return.

Where we fly

  • Business hubs: Teterboro, White Plains, Morristown, Farmingdale, Bedford, Washington Dulles, Chicago Midway, Dallas Love and Addison, Houston Hobby, Van Nuys, Santa Ana, Boeing Field, Denver Centennial.

  • Point-to-point shuttles: Teterboro-Martha's Vineyard, Teterboro-Chicago Midway, White Plains-Boca Raton, Van Nuys-Las Vegas, Denver Centennial-Aspen, Opa-locka-Nassau and more.

  • Ski and mountain season: Aspen, Vail/Eagle, Jackson Hole, Sun Valley, Montrose (Telluride), Hayden (Steamboat), Bozeman (Big Sky), Truckee (Lake Tahoe). Mustang and Premier do not fly to mountain airports, and Aspen's 95 ft wingspan limit keeps the Gulfstreams out (the Longitude flies KIND-Aspen).

  • Florida and the Caribbean: Naples, Key West, Boca Raton, Sarasota, Opa-locka, Miami Executive, San Juan, St Thomas, Montego Bay, Punta Cana, Cancun, Bermuda, St Maarten.

  • Northeast summer: Nantucket, Martha's Vineyard, Providence, Portland (Maine), Hartford.

  • Sports and college charters from KIND: Detroit, Columbus, Champaign, State College, Madison, Lincoln, Minneapolis, St Louis, Memphis, Jacksonville, Houston, San Antonio, Denver, Phoenix, Salt Lake City, Seattle (Boeing Field and Sea-Tac), Portland.

  • Transatlantic executive: London (Luton, Farnborough, London City via Shannon in the Mustang, CJ4 or Phenom 300E), Paris Le Bourget, Geneva, Zurich, Milan Linate, Madrid, Munich, Nice.

  • Cargo: Miami, Newark, Oakland, Ontario, Fort Worth Alliance, Mexico City, Anchorage, Stansted, Leipzig, Liege.

Picking a flight

  1. Open Book Flight or Dispatch in Phoenix and filter by your aircraft or by tag (executive, ski, sports-charter, college, group-charter, international, cargo-charter, short-hop, positioning).

  2. Start where you are: your next flight departs from the airport where your last PIREP ended. Use Jumpseat in Phoenix to move somewhere else.

  3. Check the fleet list on the route: only those fleets may fly it.

  4. Not sure where to start? See Featured Routes for the Charter of the Week.

Crossroads Executive & Charter is a fictional virtual airline for flight simulation. Routes are for simulation only.
