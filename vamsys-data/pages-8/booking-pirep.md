# Booking and PIREP Guide

## 1. Book

1. Log in to Phoenix (the vAMSYS pilot portal) and open **Book Flight** or **Dispatch**.
2. Choose a route that departs from your current location and lists your aircraft.
3. Select the aircraft and book it. A booking stays valid for 24 hours.

## 2. Plan

- Generate the SimBrief flight plan from the Dispatch page (use the CRX callsign, for example CRX183).
- Check the weather and the runway in use. Mountain airports (Aspen, Vail, Jackson Hole, Sun Valley, Montrose, Hayden, Bozeman, Truckee) need performance planning: density altitude matters.
- Fuel: plan reserves plus an alternate. The planning ranges in the Fleet Guide already include a margin, but winter headwinds westbound are real.

## 3. Fly

- Start the ACARS client (Pegasus) before you push back and keep it running until you are parked with engines off.
- Fly the routing you filed. Online on VATSIM or offline are both welcome; follow network rules when online.
- Land gently: the landing rate is recorded on every PIREP.

## 4. File

- Pegasus files the PIREP automatically at the end of the flight. Add a comment if something unusual happened (diversion, technical problem, go-around).
- PIREPs are reviewed by staff. Accepted PIREPs count toward your rank hours and flights.

## Airline rules (accepted when you join)

- Depart from your current location. To start somewhere else, use **Jumpseat** in Phoenix first; never slew or reposition the aircraft in the sim.
- No slew, pause-to-skip or time acceleration in flight.
- Use the correct aircraft type for the booked fleet.
- Be courteous to other pilots and controllers, and follow VATSIM or IVAO rules when flying online.
- Do not file PIREPs for flights you did not fly.

*Crossroads Executive & Charter is a fictional virtual airline for flight simulation.*
