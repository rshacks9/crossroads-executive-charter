# Fleet Guide

Crossroads Executive & Charter flies 18 fleets from two Indianapolis airports. The rule is simple: **light and mid-size jets live at Indianapolis Executive (KTYQ); everything larger lives at Indianapolis International (KIND).** Every route is assigned to the fleets that can fly it, so if a fleet appears on a route you can book it.
