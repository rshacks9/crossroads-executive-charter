# Route Guide

Every Crossroads flight uses the callsign **CRX** plus a three-digit number, and the flight number **CX** plus the same digits (CRX183 is flight CX183). Odd numbers fly outbound from Indianapolis or the first city of a pair; even numbers fly the return.

## Callsign blocks

| Block | Division | Base | Typical aircraft |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| CRX101-199 | Executive, light and mid-size jets | KTYQ | Mustang, Premier, Learjet, CJ4, Hawker, Citation X |
| CRX201-299 | Group charter | KIND | E175, E190, 757, A220 |
| CRX301-399 | Sports and college charter | KIND | E175, E190, 757, A220, A340 |
| CRX401-499 | Executive, large-cabin jets | KIND | Longitude, G650, G700, A220, A340, A380 |
| CRX501-599 | International and transatlantic | KIND | Longitude, G650, G700, A340, A380 |
| CRX601-699 | Cargo charter | KIND | A300F, MD-11F, 777F |
| CRX701-799 | Executive network expansion (KTYQ and point-to-point) | KTYQ | Light and mid-size jets |

## Where we fly

- **Business hubs:** Teterboro, White Plains, Morristown, Farmingdale, Bedford, Washington Dulles, Chicago Midway, Dallas Love and Addison, Houston Hobby, Van Nuys, Santa Ana, Boeing Field.
- **Ski and mountain season:** Aspen, Vail/Eagle, Jackson Hole, Sun Valley, Montrose (Telluride), Hayden (Steamboat), Bozeman (Big Sky), Truckee (Lake Tahoe). Mustang and Premier do not fly to mountain airports.
- **Florida and the Caribbean:** Naples, Key West, Boca Raton, Sarasota, Opa-locka, Miami Executive, San Juan, St Thomas, Montego Bay, Punta Cana, Cancun, Bermuda, St Maarten.
- **Northeast summer:** Nantucket, Providence, Portland (Maine), Hartford.
- **Sports and college charters from KIND:** Detroit, Columbus, Champaign, State College, Madison, Lincoln, Minneapolis, St Louis, Memphis, Jacksonville, Houston, San Antonio, Denver, Phoenix, Salt Lake City, Seattle (Boeing Field and Sea-Tac), Portland.
- **Transatlantic executive:** London (Luton, Farnborough, London City via Shannon), Paris Le Bourget, Geneva, Zurich, Milan Linate, Madrid, Munich, Nice.
- **Cargo:** Miami, Newark, Oakland, Ontario, Fort Worth Alliance, Mexico City, Anchorage, Stansted, Leipzig, Liege.

## Picking a flight

1. Open **Book Flight** or **Dispatch** in Phoenix and filter by your aircraft or by tag (executive, ski, sports-charter, college, group-charter, international, cargo-charter, short-hop).
2. Start where you are: your next flight departs from the airport where your last PIREP ended. Use Jumpseat in Phoenix to move somewhere else.
3. Check the fleet list on the route: only those fleets may fly it.

*Crossroads Executive & Charter is a fictional virtual airline for flight simulation. Routes are for simulation only.*
